How every portal works

  1. You search a topic.
  2. The portal finds the facts on trusted sources; no source means no article.
  3. AI writes a plain-language guide from those facts and links them.
  4. It is translated into Tamil, Sinhala, Hindi and Bengali.
  5. A short narrated video can be made in any language.
  6. An administrator checks it and marks it Verified.
General health information only. Not a substitute for professional medical advice.
